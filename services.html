Curated Discipline 04
Methodology

The 5-Step Bespoke Cadence

An unhurried, rigorous progression from initial audit to final white-glove installation. We orchestrate each phase with architectural precision.

01
Phase I

Spatial Audit & Discovery

Laser volumetric surveying, sun path mapping, structural diagnosis, and deep lifestyle cadence dialogue.

Duration: 2-3 Weeks
02
Phase II

Concept & Materiality

Atmospheric mood narratives, tactile physical mood trays (stone, timber, metal), and initial schematic layout proposals.

Duration: 4-5 Weeks
03
Phase III

Technical Design & 3D

Full construction tender packages, bespoke joinery blueprints, lighting schematics, and high-fidelity photorealistic renders.

Duration: 6-8 Weeks
04
Phase IV

Procurement & Fabrication

Artisanal commission tracking, factory inspections, international freight logistics, and specialized bonded warehousing.

Duration: Concurrent
05
Phase V

White-Glove Turnkey Reveal

On-site assembly, art hanging, fine linen dressing, scent curation, and a comprehensive owner spatial manual handover.

Duration: 1-2 Weeks
