The Studio Narrative

Crafting spaces that endure long after novelty dissolves.

Founded in Paris along the banks of the Seine, Home Decor emerged from a deliberate rejection of synthetic acceleration. We hold the conviction that true luxury is not marked by ornamentation, but by spatial stillness, light choreography, and the authentic touch of raw, unvarnished natural matter.

Each bespoke commission is conceived as a one-of-one monograph. We forage tactile mediums across Europe and the Mediterranean: quarry-carved Roman travertine, hand-planed French white oak aged by ambient cellar moisture, Belgian natural unbleached flax linens, and lost-wax cast bronze elements sculpted in small artisanal foundries.

Material Honesty

We never disguise one medium as another. Wood remains unlacquered to breathe; stone retains its prehistoric veining and micro-fissures; metals are unsealed to invite the natural fingerprints of time.
